Contact our Parent Committee Chair or your scout’s Scoutmaster to find out what training you need to lead Troop 506B or 506G events. Examples of training opportunities include:

- Youth Protection Training (required)
- Scoutmaster Training
- Assistant Scoutmaster Training
- Parent Leadership Training
- Outdoor Leadership Skills
- General & Wilderness First Aid
- CPR & Rescue Breathing

#### Youth Protection Training

**Youth Protection Training (YPT) is mandatory for all adults before participating in Troop 506B or 506G activities**. YPT certification is available online at [Scouting.org](https://www.scouting.org/training/youth-protection/). Note that new users must first create an account using their nine-digit BSA membership ID number in order to record their training completions. Contact our Parent Committee Chair if you do not know your BSA ID number.

#### Onsite Training

The San Diego Imperial County Council offers local in-person training and certifications from expert instructors throughout San Diego County. Visit the council website for a list and schedule of courses at [SDICBSA.org](http://www.sdicbsa.org/training).

#### Online Training

Online training and certifications are available at [Scouting.org](https://www.scouting.org/training/). Note that new users must first create an account using their nine-digit BSA membership ID number in order to record their training completions. Contact our Parent Committee Chair if you do not know your BSA ID number.
