## BSA Medical Forms

Participation in scouting events requires medical releases from participating scouts and parents.

- **Forms A & B** are required for all activities lasting less than 72 hours.
- **Form C** is required for all activities lasting 72 hours or longer.

[Follow this link to download](https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/ahmr/), then send your completed medical forms to our [Medical Forms Coordinator](/leaders/adult-leadership/).
