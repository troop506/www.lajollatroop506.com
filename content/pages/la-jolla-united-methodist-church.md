For over 50 Years, [La Jolla United Methodist Church](https://www.lajollaunitedmethodist.org/) chartered Troop 506, and while our troop is now chartered through [VFW Post 5895](/charter-organization/), La Jolla United Methodist Church continues to be our home, where we meet and store our gear. We are very grateful to the entire community of La Jolla United Methodist Church for their support.
